Promsell is a free, offline-first point-of-sale app for small shops, market stalls, and local merchants in Thailand.

**Core Features:**
• **Quick Sales** — Cart, discounts, VAT modes, multi-tender checkout.
• **Inventory** — Stock tracking, adjustments, and low-stock awareness.
• **Receipts & PromptPay** — Share PDF receipts (not a tax invoice). Generate PromptPay QR offline.
• **Offline-First** — Core POS works without internet. Data stays on your device (SQLCipher at rest).
• **Thai + English** — Switch language anytime.
• **Drafts & Daily Close** — Park bills and close the day.
• **Backup** — Encrypted export (recommended). Same-device restore only; no cloud multi-device restore in Community Edition.

**Perfect for:** grocery, cafés, food stalls, and small retail that need a fast cash register on a phone or tablet.

No subscription. No cloud lock-in. Open source **AGPL-3.0** — source: https://github.com/teeprakorn1/promsell-pos-ce

Receipts are sales receipts, not official tax invoices.
